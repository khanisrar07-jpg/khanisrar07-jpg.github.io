About

DBGFM · Univ. of Swat
IK

I am an MPhil scholar in Biotechnology at the Department of Biotechnology & Genetic Engineering, Fazaia Medical (DBGFM), University of Swat, working at the intersection of plant tissue culture, recombinant protein expression, and analytical phytochemistry.

My research spans three directions: regenerating oilseed rape (Brassica napus) through in vitro tissue culture, cloning and expressing a thermophilic β-glucosidase for lignocellulosic biofuel production, and profiling phenolics, flavonoids, antioxidant activity (DPPH), and artemisinin in Artemisia callus lines enhanced with silver nanoparticles (AgNPs).

Outside the lab, I explore the valleys, meadows and passes of Swat — from Kalam to Mahodand — with the same curiosity I bring to a petri dish.
